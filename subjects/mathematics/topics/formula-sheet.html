01. Arithmetic

Ratios, percentages, and rates

Percentage

Express a part as a percentage of the whole.

\(\frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}}\times100\%\)

Percentage change

Compare a change with the original value.

\(\frac{\text{New}-\text{Original}}{\text{Original}}\times100\%\)

Proportion

Equivalent ratios have equal cross-products.

\(\frac{a}{b}=\frac{c}{d}\Rightarrow ad=bc\)

Speed

Average speed over a journey.

\(\text{Speed}=\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}\)

02. Algebra

Equations and identities

Quadratic formula

Solve \(ax^2+bx+c=0\), where \(a\ne0\).

\(x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\)

Difference of squares

Factor a difference between two squares.

\(a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)\)

Square of a sum

Expand a binomial square.

\((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)

Square of a difference

Expand a binomial difference.

\((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)

Product of powers

For the same nonzero base.

\(a^m a^n=a^{m+n}\)

Power of a power

Raise a power to another power.

\((a^m)^n=a^{mn}\)

03. Geometry

Perimeter, area, surface area, and volume

Rectangle

Length \(l\), width \(w\).

\(A=lw,\quad P=2(l+w)\)

Triangle

Base \(b\), perpendicular height \(h\).

\(A=\frac12 bh\)

Circle

Radius \(r\), diameter \(d=2r\).

\(A=\pi r^2,\quad C=2\pi r\)

Pythagorean theorem

For a right-angled triangle.

\(a^2+b^2=c^2\)

Cuboid

Length \(l\), width \(w\), height \(h\).

\(V=lwh\)

Sphere

Radius \(r\).

\(V=\frac43\pi r^3,\quad S=4\pi r^2\)

Cylinder

Radius \(r\), height \(h\).

\(V=\pi r^2h\)

Cone

Circular base radius \(r\), perpendicular height \(h\).

\(V=\frac13\pi r^2h\)

04. Trigonometry

Ratios and identities

Basic ratios

Relative to angle \(\theta\) in a right triangle.

\(\sin\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}\)
\(\cos\theta=\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}\)
\(\tan\theta=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\)

Pythagorean identity

A fundamental relationship between sine and cosine.

\(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1\)

Sine of a sum

Expand the sine of two added angles.

\(\sin(A+B)=\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B\)

Cosine of a sum

Expand the cosine of two added angles.

\(\cos(A+B)=\cos A\cos B-\sin A\sin B\)

05. Coordinate geometry

Points, lines, and circles

Distance between points

Points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\).

\(d=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\)

Midpoint

Find the centre of a line segment.

\(\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)\)

Slope of a line

For two points with different \(x\)-coordinates.

\(m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}\)

Slope-intercept form

A straight line with slope \(m\) and intercept \(c\).

\(y=mx+c\)

Circle equation

Centre \((h,k)\), radius \(r\).

\((x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2\)

06. Sequences and series

Arithmetic and geometric patterns

Arithmetic sequence

First term \(a\), common difference \(d\).

\(a_n=a+(n-1)d\)

Arithmetic series sum

Sum of the first \(n\) terms.

\(S_n=\frac n2[2a+(n-1)d]\)

Geometric sequence

First term \(a\), common ratio \(r\).

\(a_n=ar^{n-1}\)

Finite geometric sum

For \(r\ne1\).

\(S_n=a\frac{1-r^n}{1-r}\)

07. Calculus

Derivatives and integrals

Power rule

Differentiate a power of \(x\).

\(\frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}\)

Sum rule

Differentiate a sum of functions.

\((f+g)'=f'+g'\)

Product rule

Differentiate a product of functions.

\((fg)'=f'g+fg'\)

Chain rule

Differentiate a composite function.

\(\frac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))g'(x)\)

Power rule for integration

For \(n\ne-1\).

\(\int x^n\,dx=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\)

Definite integral

If \(F'=f\), then:

\(\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a)\)

08. Probability

Events and counting

Classical probability

For equally likely outcomes.

\(P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|S|}\)

Complement rule

Probability that event \(A\) does not occur.

\(P(A^c)=1-P(A)\)

Addition rule

For any two events.

\(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\)

Conditional probability

When \(P(B)>0\).

\(P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}\)

Permutations

Arrange \(r\) items selected from \(n\), where order matters.

\({}_nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}\)

Combinations

Choose \(r\) items from \(n\), where order does not matter.

\({}_nC_r=\frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}\)

09. Statistics

Central tendency and spread

Arithmetic mean

Average of \(n\) observations.

\(\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x_i}{n}\)

Range

Difference between the largest and smallest values.

\(\text{Range}=x_{\max}-x_{\min}\)

Population variance

Mean squared deviation from population mean \(\mu\).

\(\sigma^2=\frac{\sum(x_i-\mu)^2}{N}\)

Sample variance

Unbiased estimator of population variance.

\(s^2=\frac{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1}\)

Standard deviation

Square root of the variance.

\(\sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2}\)

Interquartile range

Spread of the middle half of the data.

\(\mathrm{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1\)

Important: Formulas may depend on their assumptions. For example, classical probability assumes equally likely outcomes, the sample variance formula uses \(n-1\), and trigonometric ratios depend on the angle and triangle context. Check definitions and conditions before applying a formula.
