What to notice

  • Higher temperature increases the model rate.
  • Higher concentration increases collision frequency.
  • Greater surface area speeds up many reactions involving solids.

Scientific note: This is a conceptual simulation. The relative rate is calculated using a simplified illustrative formula; it is not a universal law or a prediction for a specific chemical reaction. In real experiments, the effect of temperature depends on the reaction and its activation energy. Never mix unknown chemicals or heat chemicals without appropriate supervision and safety equipment.
