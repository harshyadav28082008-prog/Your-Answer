Theory and formula

For an ideal simple pendulum undergoing small oscillations, its time period depends on its length and the local gravitational acceleration.

T ≈ 2π√(L/g)

T = time period · L = length · g = gravitational acceleration

Frequency is the number of complete oscillations per second. For this approximation, changing the bob's mass does not change the calculated time period.

Experiment procedure

  1. Set gravity to 9.81 m/s² and choose a length.
  2. Record the calculated time period and frequency.
  3. Change the length while keeping gravity constant. Compare the results.
  4. Restore the original length and change gravity.
  5. Try a different starting angle and observe the swing. The formula is an approximation, especially at larger angles.
