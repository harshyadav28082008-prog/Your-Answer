Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. Investigate how light intensity, carbon dioxide availability, and temperature can affect the rate of this process.

Key observations

  • Light is the energy source for photosynthesis.
  • Low carbon dioxide can limit the rate.
  • Temperature affects the enzymes involved in the process.
  • Increasing one factor may not help if another factor is limiting.
