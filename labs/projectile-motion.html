Projectile motion equations

T = 2v sinθ / g
H = v² sin²θ / 2g
R = v² sin(2θ) / g

T = flight time · H = maximum height · R = horizontal range

Experiment procedure

  1. Start with a speed of 20 m/s and an angle of 45°.
  2. Launch the projectile and observe its trajectory.
  3. Change only the launch angle and compare the range.
  4. Try different initial speeds.
  5. Change gravity and observe how the trajectory changes.
  6. Record several observations and compare the results.
