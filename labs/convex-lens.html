Lens formula

For a thin convex lens, the relationship between focal length, object distance and image distance can be represented by the lens formula.

1/f = 1/v − 1/u

f = focal length · u = object distance · v = image distance

Magnification is calculated from the ratio of image height to object height and can also be expressed as m = v/u using the corresponding sign convention.

Experiment procedure

  1. Set the focal length to 10 cm.
  2. Place the object beyond 2F.
  3. Observe the position and size of the image.
  4. Move the object closer to the lens and observe the changes.
  5. Place the object between F and the lens.
  6. Observe the formation of a virtual, upright and magnified image.
