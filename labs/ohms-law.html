Ohm's Law

V = IR

Voltage = Current × Resistance

Current can therefore be calculated using I = V/R. For this ideal experiment, resistance is treated as constant while voltage is changed.

Voltage–current relationship

The graph shows the observations recorded during the experiment. For a constant resistance, voltage and current have a linear relationship.

Experiment procedure

  1. Set the resistance to a fixed value.
  2. Change the voltage gradually.
  3. Observe the corresponding current.
  4. Record several observations.
  5. Compare the voltage and current values.
  6. Observe the approximately straight-line relationship.
