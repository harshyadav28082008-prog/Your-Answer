Understanding the vector

This vector moves 3 units along X, 2 units along Y and 4 units along Z. Its magnitude is the straight-line distance from the origin to the vector's endpoint.

The three components are perpendicular to one another. By the three-dimensional Pythagorean theorem, their squares add to the square of the vector's magnitude.
