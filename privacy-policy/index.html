Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains what information may be collected when you visit Your Answer, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy applies to the Your Answer website and its learning features, including subject pages, interactive 3D experiences, virtual labs, and coding tools.

It describes the site's use of Google Analytics and the possible use of Google AdSense or other advertising services if advertising is enabled.

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, the hosting provider and services used by the website may process technical information needed to deliver, protect, and understand the website.

Depending on the service and its configuration, this may include your IP address, browser and device information, pages requested, approximate location derived from network information, and the date and time of a request.

The site does not require you to create an account to browse its public learning pages. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through interactive features or code examples.

3. Google Analytics

Your Answer uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited and how the site is performing.

Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information about your browser, device, and interactions with the site.

Google processes analytics information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can read the Google Privacy Policy and Google Analytics information .

4. Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that service and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display, measure, and personalise advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and laws.

Advertising partners may collect information about visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertising or measure advertising performance.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps . You can also manage Google's advertising preferences through My Ad Center .

Your Answer does not control the cookies or data practices of third-party advertising providers.

5. How information is used

Information processed through the site may be used to:

  • Operate and secure the website.
  • Understand website usage and improve learning resources.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Support advertising where advertising is enabled.

Your Answer does not sell visitors' personal information.

6. Third-party services and links

The website may link to or rely on third-party services, such as Google services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Review their policies before using them. Your Answer is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Your choices

  • You can adjust or clear cookies through your browser settings.
  • Blocking cookies may affect some website or third-party features.
  • You can use Google's controls to manage advertising personalisation.
  • You can use available browser and device privacy controls to limit certain forms of tracking.

8. Children's privacy

Your Answer is an educational website for a general audience. The site does not intentionally request personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the internet and the privacy settings available in their browsers and devices.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, analytics, advertising, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact

A dedicated contact method will be published on the website when one is established. This policy will be updated when a direct contact channel becomes available.
